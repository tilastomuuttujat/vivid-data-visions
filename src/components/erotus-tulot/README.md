# Erotus · tulot

Separate evidence-based prototype at `/erotus-tulot` (Pages: `/#/erotus-tulot`). The original `/erotus` stays unchanged. The app and standalone export share the same component.

The whole is the **nominal monthly disposable income of one Labore example household**, not society's wellbeing. The selected part is a signed accounting contribution in euros. Choosing a household changes perspective, not the alleged culprit. No arbitrary factor weights, population extrapolation, causal scores or invented annual data.

Source: existing `planeetta/household-example-data.json`; each publication vintage remains separate. The 2025 point remains labelled a source-vintage forecast even after 2025 has passed. Verifiable source PDF, page, publication and calculation dates appear in the UI. The original PDF extraction script remains the data update path.

Identity: net = gross + other transfers − taxes + source rounding residual.
Contribution: signed component in final year minus signed component in base year.
Comparison: source net minus contribution. This holds only that component at its baseline euro amount, without recalculating taxes, benefits or behaviour. It is not a policy counterfactual. Brutto can contain taxable benefits; tax euros do not identify changes in tax rates. No household population weights are supplied, so household examples are not summed into a national total.

Curves use endpoint-bounded cubic segments. Only source points are labelled; curves have no evidentiary meaning between observations. Forecast segments are dashed; source points and an accessible exact-value table remain available. The hatch shows an arithmetic difference, not an uncertainty interval.

To extend to other themes, first define a measurable whole, its reconciled parts, units, denominators and coverage. Causal claims require a separate identified study or policy simulation with explicit assumptions. Do not restore editorial impact weights under empirical labels.

Validation: conservation over all households, source vintages and baselines; sign and compensation regression; absent-year rejection; UI changes of household, component, period and vintage; standalone production build. No statistical confidence intervals are fabricated for model examples.
